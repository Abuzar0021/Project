Questions

What happens after the trial?

You pick a plan or drop to Free. We email you three days before the trial ends, and nothing is charged without a card on file.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from Settings in one click. Yearly plans are refunded for the unused months.

How does the voice profile learn?

From the drafts you write in Margin, plus any past writing you choose to upload. You can reset it whenever you want.

Do you offer discounts?

Students and non-profits get Pro at half price. Write to us from your school or organisation email.
